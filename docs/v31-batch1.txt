Scope
- Colombia Primera A: 20-team Finalización record, linked season-specific club profiles, exact league/cup association for América de Cali's reviewed DIMAYOR announcement.
- Bundesliga: 18-team record, source-recorded completed results, club profiles, exact existing approved artwork.
- NHL: 32 teams, source-defined Eastern/Western conferences and four divisions, hockey columns and division-bound rankings, linked franchise profiles.
- Shared mobile-first header, local conference filter, standings, source-aware results, participants, relevant news, source rights and related competitions.

Source evidence (public database snapshots inspected 24 October 2026)
Colombia: Wikimedia, 2026 Finalizacion, Torneo Finalización, 20 teams; cutoff 2026-10-21. CC BY-SA 4.0 adaptation with exact source revisions and contributor links retained.
Bundesliga: OpenLigaDB, 2026/27, 18 teams; cutoff 2026-10-18T15:30:00Z. Existing approved OpenLigaDB licence/attribution retained. Only completed scored events before that cutoff are promoted as recorded results.
NHL: Wikimedia, 2026/27, 32 teams; cutoff 2026-10-20. Four divisions, source-defined conferences. No inferred overall rank, playoff qualification, regulation wins or unverified fixtures.
These are dated snapshots, not live coverage. Source cutoffs, underlying values and publication records are not modified by this batch.

Identity and rights
Uses the existing locally hosted identity registry with reviewed Commons PD-textlogo declarations, provenance hashes and editorial-only use. Trademark rights are not claimed.
Bundesliga and approved team artwork are integrated into headers, standings, team directory and recorded matchups. América de Cali's approved wordmark is reused for its exact league/cup identity.
Missing approved artwork remains explicitly typographic; no invented crest, graphic hotlink or new asset-rights assumption. NHL artwork and most Colombian club crests remain a rights/asset coverage gap, not falsely marked complete.
Exact league/cup aliases connect América without matching similarly named clubs or other sports. Existing source IDs, season-scoped URLs and canonical metadata remain stable.

Machine-readable evidence: output/v3-qa/v31/batch1/route-audit-before.json (ignored local artifact, no credentials).
