V3.9-B: bounded newsroom throughput

Ordinary Production cron cycles may process at most two different eligible stories.
The existing six-hour schedule and 180-second route remain unchanged. Before each
story the queue is ranked using the latest 24-hour sport/source publication mix.
Each story gets the complete EN -> ES/PT -> independent audit/correction workflow,
with at most five model calls and its own $0.02 atomic durable reservation.
Validation and separately authorized canaries remain limited to one story.

Daily conservative spending remains $0.10 and monthly spending remains $3.
Reservations are retained after failures; they are never refunded from uncertain
usage. These limits permit at most five reserved story attempts per UTC day,
not a promise of five publications. Empty cycles still retain their original
reservation. No new source, subscription, infrastructure or affiliate is enabled.

A second story only starts with at least 110 seconds left inside a 165-second
application deadline. Per-call lease/pause and deadline checks remain mandatory.
Text/audit rejections quarantine that candidate while another can proceed. Shared
model transport, rate or billing failures stop further calls. No factual/audit
rejection is automatically approved. Candidate identity and reservation are checked
transactionally before generation. Published stories survive a later batch failure.

Owner run details show per-story audits, tokens, measured cost and batch stop reason.
Older single-story run history remains readable. No private drafts enter public
responses or release documents.

Migration 076 widens only the existing per-run reservation constraint from 20000
to 40000 microUSD. It is repeatable, with five-second lock and 30-second statement
timeouts. Apply through the normal release flow before the new runner is deployed.
Do not rerun previous migrations. Existing records are preserved. An application
rollback to the previous READY deployment remains compatible with this wider
constraint; no destructive database rollback is required.

Acceptance: isolated PostgreSQL verifies repeatability, preservation, concurrent
single-use reservations, reconnects, daily/monthly hard stops, owner quarantine
races, emergency pause, expired leases, and old single-story compatibility.
No synthetic data or source/model requests are used in Production acceptance.
Real multi-story and third-sport publication remain subject to normal cron time,
source freshness, eligibility and independent editorial approval.
