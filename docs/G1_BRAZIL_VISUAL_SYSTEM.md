# G1 Brazil visual system

G1 retains LivaSports' deep navy identity and system font stack. Teal is the primary action color; restrained green, gold and blue distinguish Brazil context, selected navigation and competition groups. Live status uses the established pink with text, without aggressive motion.

The header has a stronger original LivaSports mark, clearer active navigation and 44px locale controls. Mobile keeps navigation on an accessible second row. Scoreboard counts sit above the competition list, and compact team/score rows use stronger hierarchy, subtle dividers and fewer nested surfaces. Brazil ordering still comes from the canonical registry; all 34 competitions remain available.

Match Center strengthens the hero score and crests. Scheduled matches with no score show their real localized kickoff time. Crests request suitable image dimensions instead of enlarging tiny thumbnails. Section navigation reflects the visible section and preserves normal anchor and keyboard behavior. Events, statistics, lineups, standings and odds share readable spacing and typography.

Team and player pages improve identity, current context, facts, form and statistic hierarchy. Existing provider images and real initial/shield fallbacks remain. Long names wrap. Form letters remain alongside color, and unavailable data stays explicit.

Odds and slip comparisons retain M5–M8 semantics. Tabular figures align prices; best-price treatment is restrained; affiliate CTAs are secondary. The mobile slip remains a bottom sheet, and desktop uses a compact 368px panel. Focus outlines, reachable controls, retained selections and reduced motion remain supported.

## Verification

Primary target: 390px. Required widths: 375, 390, 430, 768 and 1440. Internal before/after evidence is kept in ignored `output/g1-evidence/`; it is not shipped as application imagery.

The rendered matrix covers BR/MX home, scheduled and finished Match Centers, Brazil and long-name teams, rich and partial players, empty/current/partial/stale slips and local labeled current-odds CTA replay. Real data is distinguished from controlled local replay. Canonical URLs, hreflang, sitemap, SSR content and provider-call boundaries are preserved.

No remote font, advertising SDK, new runtime package, fabricated player photo or generated operator creative was introduced. The performance and final release results are recorded in [G1_COMMERCIAL_QA.md](G1_COMMERCIAL_QA.md) and the G1 report.
