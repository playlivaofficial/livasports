LivaSports V3.4-A — data integrity and operational coverage
Review date: 26 October 2026. Baseline: 31c750947f94d36122e944f32db7e9168708b2d8.

Scope and evidence
- Owner sports coverage is derived from current database publications, reviewed candidates and refresh state. The 64-entry catalog supplies names/routes only, not a claim of operating coverage.
- Public directory cards distinguish recent verified, dated and historical publications and expose the actual source date. Retrieval does not reset freshness. Revision timestamps remain explicitly unconfirmed result cutoffs.
- Owner coverage is behind the existing authenticated, private/no-store boundary. No draft content or refresh lease is included in the coverage projection. The existing private editor remains unchanged.
- Refresh failures preserve published snapshots. Automatic refresh now rejects source-cutoff regression and incomparable source/season/context before mutation. Same-cutoff corrections remain possible through the existing atomic validation/audit path.
- The schema does not record a distinct last-failure timestamp: this field is null, not inferred from last-attempt. Last success, last attempt, sanitized last error, next due time and enabled/eligible states remain distinct. Alerts are dashboard alerts, not a claim of external notification delivery.
- No new migration, subscription, paid sports provider or automatic source policy.
